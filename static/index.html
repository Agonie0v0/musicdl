curl_cffi 用于模拟浏览器指纹，部分风控严格的音源需要开启。
高级 · 按音源覆盖（JSON，键为音源名，如 "NeteaseMusicClient"）
init_music_clients_cfg 客户端配置覆盖
例：{"NeteaseMusicClient": {"work_dir": "netease_out", "search_size_per_source": 10, "default_search_cookies": {"MUSIC_U": "..."}}}
clients_threadings 线程数覆盖
例：{"QQMusicClient": 8, "GDStudioMusicClient": 10}
requests_overrides 请求参数覆盖
例：{"YouTubeMusicClient": {"proxies": {"http": "http://127.0.0.1:7890", "https": "http://127.0.0.1:7890"}}}
search_rules 搜索规则
例：{"NeteaseMusicClient": {"ext": "flac"}}
